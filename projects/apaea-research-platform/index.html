01 / DIGITAL RESEARCH INFRASTRUCTURE

One ecosystem.
Many important roles.

One platform, several audiences: members, authors, reviewers, event participants and administrators. The architectural story is about reducing friction across the research lifecycle.

01

Membership & identity

Individual/institutional membership records, registration and dashboards with payment-handling code.

02

Editorial operations

Conference and general paper submissions, reviewer assignment, decisions, feedback and proofreading requests.

03

Research publishing

Journal and issue management, RePEc-related archive metadata, working papers and research datasets.

04

Events & discovery

Conference publishing, event registration, partner pages, news, galleries and public information management.
